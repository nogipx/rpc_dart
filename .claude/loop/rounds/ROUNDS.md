## The 251-265 gap, explained (curate after round 327)

`loop.py lint` has warned about a hole between 250 and 266 on every run for
around seventy rounds, and nobody had written down why. It is not lost data.

Those fifteen rounds were **one investigation** — B-22, the paused consumer that
never repaid the connection pool — and their findings were written into the LEAD
rather than into round files. The commits are all in git
(`docs(loop): round 263 …`, `264`, `265`), and
`../backlog/archive/B-22-paused-consumer-never-repays-the-pool.md` names 251, 252, 253,
254, 256, 262, 263, 264 and 265 by number: five witnesses that failed to observe
the defect and one reading that explained why.

So the gap is a record-keeping choice, not a gap in knowledge. Left as-is — the
content is reachable and renumbering would break every inbound reference — and
`lint`'s warning stands as a pointer to this note rather than to a mystery.
